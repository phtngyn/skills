name: sharpen
description: "Sharpen a task before starting: the expert approach, the user's intended outcome, and the right level of specification. Use for $sharpen."
---

Before starting, consider how an expert would approach the task: authoritative sources, effective methods, and an independent check of the result. Identify what the user is trying to achieve and whether the task is over- or underspecified. Question incidental constraints that do not serve the goal; preserve explicit user constraints and leave choices that belong to the user open. Fold in only what improves the work within its authorized scope, then do the work. Include a check independent of the implementation's own claims, proportionate to the task. Do not stop at handing back a rewritten prompt.
